# Better Bookmark 🔖

> A modern, beautiful new tab experience that transforms how you manage and access your bookmarks.

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

## ✨ What is Better Bookmark?

Better Bookmark replaces your browser's default new tab page with a powerful, elegant bookmark management interface. It's designed to help you organize, search, and access your bookmarks faster and more efficiently than ever before.

## 🎯 Key Features

### 📊 **Flexible Display Modes**
- **Grid View**: Beautiful card-based layout with favicon icons for quick visual recognition
- **List View**: Compact, traditional list format for maximum information density
- Seamlessly switch between views based on your preference or workflow

### 🏷️ **Smart Tag System**
- Organize bookmarks with custom tags
- Multi-tag support for flexible categorization
- Tag-based filtering and quick search
- Visual tag management interface

### 🌳 **Intuitive Folder Navigation**
- Browse your bookmark hierarchy with a clean folder tree
- Quick access to frequently used folders
- Display bookmark counts for each folder
- Remember your last visited folder for faster access

### 🔍 **Powerful Search**
- Instant search across bookmark names and URLs
- Search through your browsing history
- Real-time results as you type
- Smart filtering by folders and tags

### 🎨 **Beautiful Themes**
- **Light Mode**: Clean, minimal design perfect for daytime browsing
- **Dark Mode**: Easy on the eyes for late-night sessions
- Automatic theme switching based on your preference

### ⚡ **Smart Features**
- **Quick Actions**: Add, edit, or delete bookmarks without opening a new tab
- **Folder Organization**: Move bookmarks between folders with drag-and-drop ease
- **Time Widget**: Stay productive with an elegant clock display
- **Direct Browser Manager Access**: One-click access to your browser's native bookmark manager

### 🌍 **Multilingual Support**

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Install Better Bookmark from the Chrome Web Store (or your browser's extension marketplace)
2. Open a new tab to see your bookmarks beautifully organized
3. Customize your view settings, theme, and language preferences
4. Start organizing with tags and folders
5. Enjoy faster access to your favorite sites!

## 💡 Why Choose Better Bookmark?

### **Simple Yet Powerful**
We believe great tools should be intuitive. Better Bookmark gives you advanced bookmark management without the complexity.

### **Respectful of Your Privacy**
All your bookmark data stays local on your browser. We don't collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information.

## 📝 Tips & Tricks

- Use **tags** to create cross-folder collections of related bookmarks
- Enable **"Remember Last Folder"** to always return to your most-used section
- Turn on **"Show Bookmark Count"** to track how many items you've saved
- Try **keyboard navigation** for even faster browsing
- Switch between **Grid and List views** depending on your task
